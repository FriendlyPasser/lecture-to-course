Conditioning changes the reference group

Start with everyone, then keep only the people who satisfy the condition.

A conditional probability uses that smaller reference group as its denominator.

The condition tells us which group to count within.

Choose the denominator

Author-created exercise

Of 40 club members, 10 play chess. Of those 10 chess players, 4 also play tennis.

We want the probability of playing tennis given that the member plays chess.

The numerator counts members who satisfy both properties. There are four.

The denominator counts all members who satisfy the condition.

Which denominator should we use?

Use 10: only chess players belong to the reference group. The answer is 4/10.
