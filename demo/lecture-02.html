01 / THE IDEA

Does the information change the probability?

Events are when their joint probability equals the product of their individual probabilities. If P(S) > 0, an equivalent test is P(E | S) = P(E).

P(E∩S)=P(E)P(S)

The equality is the independence condition, not a formula that holds for every pair of events.
