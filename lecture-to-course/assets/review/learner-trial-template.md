# Voluntary learner trial record

Copy one record per anonymous participant and reviewed version into the local working review folder, outside the generated site. Do not add names, contact details or student numbers. Do not fabricate responses; unanswered prompts and missed follow-ups remain missing evidence. Guidance: `references/teaching-review.md` in the skill package.

## Invitation draft — user sends manually

**English**

Would you like to try a short section of this offline course and help identify unclear explanations? Participation is voluntary: you may skip any question or stop at any time. We will ask where the explanation first becomes unclear, invite you to explain an idea and try a new problem, and optionally agree a later time for a similar problem. Before starting, we will agree what responses to record under an anonymous local ID. We do not need your name, contact details or student number in the record. This is feedback on the course, not a graded assessment of you. You can choose English or Chinese and ask to see a solution whenever you want; any assistance will be recorded separately.

**简体中文**

你愿意试读这门离线课程的一小段，帮助找出讲解不清楚的地方吗？参与完全自愿，你可以跳过任何问题，也可以随时停止。我们会请你指出最先看不懂的句子或步骤，用自己的话解释一个概念，并尝试一道新题；如果你愿意，还可以约定过一段时间再做一道相似题。开始前，我们会与你确认记录哪些回答，并使用本地匿名编号；记录不需要你的姓名、联系方式或学号。这是在收集课程反馈，不是对你的评分考核。你可以选择英文或中文，也可以随时查看解答；提示和其他帮助会单独记录。

This draft is not evidence of an invitation sent or a participant recruited. Do not send messages as part of this workflow.
